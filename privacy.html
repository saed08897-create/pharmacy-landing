02النسخ الاحتياطي عبر Google Drive (ميزة اختيارية)

يوفّر التطبيق ميزة اختيارية لأخذ نسخة احتياطية من قاعدة بياناتك المحلية ورفعها إلى حساب Google Drive الخاص بك أنت (وليس إلى أي خادم نملكه). تعمل هذه الميزة فقط إذا اخترت تفعيلها بنفسك وسجّلت دخولك بحساب Google.

  • يستخدم التطبيق صلاحية Google محدودة جدًا تُسمى drive.file، والتي تسمح للتطبيق بالوصول فقط للملفات التي أنشأها هو نفسه داخل مجلد نسخ احتياطي خاص به — ولا يمكنه رؤية أو الوصول لأي ملف آخر في حساب Google Drive الخاص بك.
  • النسخة الاحتياطية تبقى ملكًا لك بالكامل، مخزّنة في حسابك أنت. نحن (فريق التطبيق) لا نملك وصولًا إليها ولا نستطيع رؤية محتواها.
  • يمكنك إلغاء تفعيل هذه الميزة أو تسجيل الخروج من حساب Google في أي وقت من داخل إعدادات التطبيق.

This document explains the privacy policy of the Mango Pharmacy (My Smart Pharmacy) application, listed on Google Play under package ID com.pharmamango.pos. We are committed to full transparency about what the app does, and does not, collect.

Quick summary: this app is designed to run entirely locally on your device. We do not operate any server that receives your business data, we do not sell or share it with any third party, and the app contains no ads or tracking tools.

01Data the app stores, and where

All data entered by the user — customer and supplier records, invoices, inventory, prices, accounts and cash records, and staff login credentials — is stored locally only, in a database on the user's own device. This data is not automatically transmitted to any internet server.

Data typeWhere it is stored
Customers, suppliers, invoices, inventory, accountsLocally on-device only
Staff username & passwordLocally on-device only, stored hashed

02Google Drive backup (optional feature)

The app offers an optional feature to back up your local database to your own Google Drive account (not to any server we operate). This only runs if you choose to enable it and sign in with your own Google account.

  • The app uses a narrow Google permission scope called drive.file, which only lets the app access files it created itself inside its own dedicated backup folder — it cannot see or access any other file in your Google Drive account.
  • The backup remains entirely your property, stored in your own account. We (the app team) have no access to it and cannot view its contents.
  • You can disable this feature or sign out of your Google account at any time from within the app's settings.

04What this app does not do

  • Does not display any advertising.
  • Does not use any analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • Does not sell or share your data with advertisers or any third party.
  • Does not collect your geographic location.
  • Does not build an advertising profile about you.

05Data deletion

Since all your data is stored locally on your device, uninstalling the app automatically deletes all of its local data. If you used the optional Google Drive backup feature, that backup remains in your own account until you delete it yourself from Google Drive.

06Children's privacy

This app is designed for managing pharmacy business operations and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13 years old.
